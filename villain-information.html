Dr.Doom

Victor Von Doom was born in Latveria, where he became a scientist and eventually was able to study in the United States. He studied alongside Reed Richards, and the two became college rivals. He eventually became the billionaire CEO of Von Doom Industries and decided to fund Reed Richards’ space mission to study a cosmic storm. Victor accompanies the team to space and is exposed to high levels of cosmic radiation when the mission goes wrong. The exposure to this radiation mutates his body and leaves him with organic metal skin, electrokinesis, and energy absorption abilities. Due to the failure of this mission, Doom loses his company and his fortune when his board of directors turns against him. Victor also becomes jealous because he is in love with Sue Storm; however, after the space mission failure, she begins to grow closer to Reed Richards. Victor blames Reed Richards for the mutation and, driven by anger, Victor murders his main corporate investor, Leonard Kinney, by blasting him with electricity. Embracing his new powers and his metal mask, he fully takes on the identity of Doctor Doom to hunt down and kill the Fantastic Four.

Loki

 Loki Laufeyson was born to Laufey, the ruler of the Frost Giants. He was born unusually small and frail compared to other Frost Giants, leading Laufey to hide him away in shame. He is found by Odin, ruler of Asgard, and adopted by him, where he grew up alongside Odin’s biological son, Thor. In Asgard, Loki possessed a brilliant mind and a strong affinity for sorcery. Because he felt like an outsider, Loki turned toward mischief, malice, and a quest for power, eventually earning the title of the God of Mischief. Due to his jealousy towards Thor, Loki betrays him and, in a quest for power, resorts to villainous ways to gain the power he seeks.

Magneto

Erik Lehnsherr was a German-Jewish Holocaust survivor who was born with the mutant ability of magnetism. During his time in Auschwitz, Erik’s family was murdered, and Sebastian Shaw took an interest in Erik and even killed his mother in front of him to see his mutant ability. After escaping, Erik has a lifelong quest for vengeance and plans to hunt down Sebastian Shaw.
