Iron Man

When wealthy genius weapon manufacturer and playboy Tony Stark visits a war zone for a weapons demonstration, he is taken hostage via a trap, where shrapnel is lodged near his heart. After a life-saving operation, which leaves him with an electromagnet in his chest,is performed by Professor Ho Yinsen, Tony is forced to build a weapon of mass destruction. With the help of Professor Yinsen, Stark creates the Iron Man suit in an attempt to escape. After his escape, Tony Stark becomes Iron Man, and with a few modifications to his suit, is able to join the Avengers. 

Captain America

During World War II, Steve Rogers was a small, sickly boy from Brooklyn desperate to do his part in the war. Due to his health, he was rejected several times; however, eventually he was accepted by Dr. Abraham Erskine into a secret military initiative led by the U.S. Government. He received the “super-soldier serum” through this initiative, transforming him into a built, conditioned soldier with enhanced strength, agility, and stamina. He becomes an integral part of World War II and is eventually frozen after bringing down an aircraft during an important mission. He is discovered and unfrozen many years later and struggles to adjust to his new life in a different time before becoming a part of the Avengers.

Wolverine

James Howlett was born in the late 19th Century and was the sickly son of James was the sickly son of wealthy estate owners John and Elizabeth Howlett. One day, the groundskeeper's exiled son, Thomas Logan, broke into the estate and killed John Howlett. This triggers James’ mutant powers: bone claws that emerge from his knuckles and the ability to heal extremely fast. In a fit of rage, James killed Thomas Logan—only to learn that Thomas was his actual biological father. He flees the estate with a young girl named Rose and adopts the name Logan. Later on, Logan becomes an integral part of the X-Men and is named the Wolverine.

Invisible Woman

Sue Storm was a scientist who went on a mission in space to study a cosmic storm with her brother, Johnny Storm, and fellow scientist Reed Richards and pilot Ben Grimm. While on their mission, their ship was exposed to high levels of cosmic radiation. Upon returning, the four friends discover that the radiation has mutated their bodies, leaving them with powers. Sue develops the power to bend light for invisibility and project solid psionic force fields. After discovering these powers, she joins the Fantastic Four along with her friends.
